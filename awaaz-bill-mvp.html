About Awaaz Bill

Voice-powered billing for Indian kirana stores. Speak in Hindi, English, or Hinglish to add items.

Tips:
• Press SPACEBAR to toggle mic on desktop
• Works offline with basic parsing
• Receipt prints at 80mm thermal width

This is an MVP prototype. Voice recognition works best on Chrome (Android or Desktop). Your API key is stored in this browser only.
